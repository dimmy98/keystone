# Keystone

**Don't lose what people know when they leave.**

Keystone is an AI that reads a company's documents and remembers who wrote each one. It shows:

1. **Who does what**: the work each person looks after, and the answers only they hold.
2. **What you'd lose if someone left**, before it happens.
3. **A handover checklist** for someone who is leaving: documents to hand over, knowledge to write down, and who should take over.
4. **Answers to any question**, with sources and a warning when only one person knows it.
5. **Tools for any file**: summaries, charts and dashboards, and conversion to PDF, JSON, CSV or Markdown.

It runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device, and it's free.

**Try it and watch the 3-minute demo: https://dimmy98.github.io/keystone/**
