    How to use Keystone

    Keystone reads a company’s documents and remembers who wrote each one. You can try it on the sample company (100 made-up staff and 102 documents) or add your own files.

    1. See who does what

    • Ask “What does Oliver Bennett do?” to see the documents a person looks after and the answers only they hold.
    • Ask “Who does what here?” for everyone, ranked by how much depends on them.

    2. Prepare for someone leaving

    • Ask “Handover checklist for Oliver Bennett” (or “Oliver Bennett is leaving”). You get a checklist of documents to hand over, knowledge to write down, questions nobody has documented, and who could take over. Tick items off as you go, or download it.
    • Ask “What if Oliver Bennett leaves?” to see every answer that would be lost.

    3. Work with any file

    • Ask “List my files”. Each file has buttons to get a summary, ask about it, see charts of its data, or convert it to PDF, JSON, CSV or Markdown.
    • Ask any question, e.g. “When are timesheets due?”. Every answer shows its sources.
    • For data, ask “Show me a dashboard” or “a chart of revenue by region”.

    Adding your own files

    • Click Add your files, the paperclip, or drag files onto the page. PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, JSON, email, web pages, text and zip files all work.
    • Keystone finds who wrote each file from its author field or an Owner: line.

    Privacy

    Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

    Add your own files

    Files are processed inside your browser and never uploaded anywhere.

      How should files look?

      The owner of each file is read automatically from its author field (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, email). You can also add an Owner: Name line, or in Markdown start the file with:

      ---
title: Billing API Runbook
owner: Priya Shah
team: Payments
---

      Add a questions.jsonl file with real questions, one per line, for example {"text": "How often is the key rotated?", "weight": 12}. Without it, questions are estimated from the documents.
